Our story

It started at Ammi’s stove.

Growing up, biryani meant Friday afternoons at home. My Ammi would start the rice and masala early, and the smell would reach the lane before lunch.

We opened this cloud kitchen so Biratnagar could taste that same plate without waiting for an invitation. Just a clean kitchen, a hot box and a rider at your door. The logo says it simply: made with love.
